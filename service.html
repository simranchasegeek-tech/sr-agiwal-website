Services

SERVICES WE PROVIDE

GST Consultation

We provide consultation in Goods and Services Tax (GST) in relation to preparation & filling of GST returns, GST Annual reconciliation & audits, Refund Applications various GST compliances, the transaction advisory & helping the client to navigate the GST regulations & the GST amendments time to time. Our team is ready to support you in the GST assessments & the GST audit conducted by the department officers.

We provide problem-solving guidance & the support systems to the client by resolving their queries, by detailed investigation of their GST data and the reconciliations of their accounting records with the respective information on GST portal.

Income Tax Consultation

Our team of experts provides services related to Income Tax annual returns filling, Tax Audits, TDS Compliances, Taxation Litigations and personalized income tax planning for all individuals, Firms, Companies, Society & Trusts etc. We also provide consultancy on future tax implications & effective tax management in respect of the potential acquisition, mergers & restructuring of corporates & others.

Our team is ever ready to help you time to time in obtaining lower tax deduction certifications and in all the scrutiny & rectification assessments and appeals filling at different levels.

Audits

An audit involves in-depth study of existing systems, procedures and controls for proper understanding to ensure that the accounts are prepared with Generally Accepted Accounting Policies. An audit also covers detection & prevention of leakages of income and suggesting corrective measures to prevent recurrence. Our expert team specialize in Income Tax Audits, GST Audits, Internal Audits, MIS Audits, System Audits etc.

We design & process our audits in a manner to ensure in-depth analysis of every process & fact of your business, which would help us to diagnose the problems in your books & business processes.

Management Consultancy

We specialize in offering the management consultancy services that help the businesses in improving their operational efficiency, increasing profitability, optimum use of their resources, and achieving sustainable growth in long-term. We use our analytical skills & our knowledge to provide the objective advice and expertise, and help an organization to develop any specialist skills that they require.

Accounting Services

Our experienced team would like to offer you their specialized services in accounting system design & implementation in any software (Tally to SAP), handling your huge & complex accounting data with real time accounting & reconciliations with taxation records. The complete & accurate accounting data would help you in your business strategy & policy making and for better coordination with your vendors & customers

Corporate Law Consultancy

Our team of qualified professionals offers highly specialized legal services includes formation of companies/LLP, various corporate compliance and secretarial services, Corporate Governance, Corporate Reorganization and Reconstructions, and helping the corporates to stay compliant with all the legal and regulatory requirements.

Business Automation

We offer customized automation solutions that streamline your business processes, provide data for decision making, increase the productivity, reduce operational costs, and trigger the alerts timely. In recent fast moving business environment and with the geographically diversified unit`s, it is getting very difficult for the entrepreneurs to manage the operations & accounting data with the traditional data tools.

So, we are here to provide you customized & smart solutions according to your business size & your budget.

Data & Analytics

We have a skilled & experienced team to provide the insightful data and various types of analytics services that help you make prompt business decisions. Our team offers the analytical & technical services along with a structured training for your routine data analysis also. We ensure that your business data should be transparent, trustworthy and quickly accessible. We also develop customized smart tools or advise tricks to analysis the data in a right direction.

Investment & Financial Planning

Our team of financial advisors provide personalized investment and financial planning services that help you achieve your long-term financial goals. Our experts provide you the best options, either for finance or for investment after a complete analysis of your financial risks, existing leverage levels, financial background and the future plans.
